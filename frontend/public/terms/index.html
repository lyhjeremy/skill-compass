What this is

SkillCompass gives calibrated practice checks across a range of professional fields, along with an estimated percentile, a resume gap report, a mock interview and a study guide. It is a self-assessment aid. It is not certification, accreditation, careers advice, financial advice, or a hiring assessment, and no employer or awarding body endorses it.

The numbers are estimates

Percentiles are computed from the people who have happened to take the same check, which is not a representative sample of any profession. They appear only once a check has at least 30 sessions, and are labelled as early data before that. Item difficulty is recalibrated continuously, so a score can move as more people answer. Treat every figure as directional. Do not present a SkillCompass result as a credential.

The AI features can be wrong

The resume gap report, the mock interview and the study guide are produced by a language model. They can be inaccurate, incomplete, or confidently mistaken. Read them as a prompt for your own thinking rather than as an assessment of your ability. Do not make an employment, financial or educational decision on their output alone.
