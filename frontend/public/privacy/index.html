Privacy

SkillCompass has no accounts, no sign-up, no advertising, and no tracking cookies. This page describes what actually happens to data, not what is legally permissible.

What stays on your device

Your quiz answers, scores, knowledge map, streak and every result page live in your browser's localStorage under the key sc_v1. They are never uploaded. Clearing your browser storage, or using the erase control on the Progress page, deletes them permanently. There is no server-side copy to request or recover.

What is sent to a server

Two things leave your device, and only these two.

Anonymous session outcomes. When you finish a check, the result (which subtopic, which items, right or wrong, and response time) is written to a database so that percentile figures can be computed across everyone who has taken the same check. No name, email, account, IP-based identifier or device fingerprint is attached. The record cannot be traced back to you, which also means it cannot be individually deleted on request.

Text you submit to an AI feature. The resume gap report, the mock interview and the study guide generator each send what you type or upload to a language model, receive a response, and discard the input. Nothing you submit to these features is written to any database, including resume text and uploaded images. If you would rather not send resume text anywhere, do not use the resume feature; every other part of the product works without it.

Children

SkillCompass is aimed at working professionals and students in higher education. It is not directed at children under 13, and it collects nothing that would identify one.
